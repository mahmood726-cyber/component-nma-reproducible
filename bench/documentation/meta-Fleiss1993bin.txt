_D_e_s_c_r_i_p_t_i_o_n:

     Meta-analysis on aspirin in preventing death after myocardial
     infarction.

     Data example in Fleiss (1993) for meta-analysis with binary
     outcomes.

_F_o_r_m_a_t:

     A data frame with the following columns:

        *_study_*  study label                             
         *_year_*  year of publication                     
        *_d.asp_*  number of deaths in aspirin group       
        *_n.asp_*  number of observations in aspirin group 
       *_d.plac_*  number of deaths in placebo group       
       *_n.plac_*  number of observations in placebo group 
      
_S_o_u_r_c_e:

     Fleiss JL (1993): The statistical basis of meta-analysis.
     _Statistical Methods in Medical Research_, *2*, 121-45

_S_e_e _A_l_s_o:

     'metabin'

_E_x_a_m_p_l_e_s:

     data(Fleiss1993bin)
     metabin(d.asp, n.asp, d.plac, n.plac, data = Fleiss1993bin,
       studlab = paste(study, year), sm = "OR", random = FALSE)
