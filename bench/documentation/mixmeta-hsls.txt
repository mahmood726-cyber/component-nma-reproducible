_D_e_s_c_r_i_p_t_i_o_n:

     This is a nationally representative, longitudinal study of more
     than 21,000 9th graders in 944 schools who will be followed
     through their secondary and postsecondary years. The data are used
     for testing whether sex, socioeconomic status and sex by
     socio-economic status interaction are predictive of the
     mathematics standardized score in each of the eight race groups.

_U_s_a_g_e:

     hsls
     
_F_o_r_m_a_t:

     A data frame with 8 observations on the following 10 variables:

        * 'race': race group.

        * 'b1, b2, b3': estimated regression coefficients for sex,
          socio-economic status and sex by socio-economic status
          interaction, respectively, on the mathematics standardized
          score.

        * 'V11, V22, V33': variances of the estimated coefficients.

        * 'V12, V13, V23': covariances of the estimated coefficients.
